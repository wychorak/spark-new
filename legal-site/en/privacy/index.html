Spark Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Spark helps you meet people nearby, date, find friends and find company for events, based on your profile, approximate location and shared interests. The app is intended only for adults (18+).

The data controller is the developer of the Spark app. Privacy contact: sparkapp@gmail.com.

What data we process

  • Account and profile: e-mail address, first name, last name or nickname, date of birth and calculated age, gender, height (if you provide it), photos, bio, interests, goals (dating, friends), social media links and search preferences.
  • Location: approximate location and city. On your public profile the location is rounded, and other people only see an approximate distance.
  • Activity in the app: likes, SparkLikes, matches, chat requests, messages, profile views, reports and blocks.
  • Events (Event Friends): events you mark as attending and the event suggestions you submit (name, city, date and time, optionally venue and description).
  • LGBT+ tag (optional): if you turn it on, your membership of the LGBT+ community, the identity terms you choose and the visibility setting. See the separate section below.
  • Notifications: your device's push notification token and your notification settings.
  • Purchases: Spark Pro status and purchase history, handled by RevenueCat and the Apple or Google store. We do not store payment card details.
  • Technical data: device and app identifiers, crash and performance data and usage statistics (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics).
  • Ads: people without Spark Pro see ads served by Google Mobile Ads. Google may process your IP address, device identifier, data about the ads shown and your interactions with them, and diagnostic data. Spark only requests non-personalized ads, and consent is collected through Google's consent form (UMP).

LGBT+ tag — sensitive data

Information about sexual orientation or gender identity is a special category of data (Article 9 GDPR). Therefore:

  • the tag is entirely voluntary and off by default;
  • we process it only on the basis of your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by turning the tag on and confirming the message in the app;
  • the tag is used only to show it on your card according to the visibility you choose and to make the “LGBT+ people only” filter work;
  • with the “LGBT+ people only” visibility the tag is not written to your public profile — the server shows it only to other people who have turned the tag on themselves;
  • we do not use the tag for advertising, analytics or profiling, and we do not share it with advertising partners;
  • you can withdraw your consent at any time by turning the tag off in your profile — we then remove it from your public profile and from your account settings.

Why we use data and on what legal basis

  • Providing the service (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, profile, matching, distance, chat, events, notifications and Spark Pro.
  • Safety and moderation (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): handling reports and blocks, filtering prohibited content, preventing abuse and fake accounts.
  • Stability and development of the app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): crash diagnostics and usage statistics.
  • Ads (according to your choice in the consent form): showing non-personalized ads to people without Spark Pro.
  • Legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): purchase accounting and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

Events and event suggestions

An event you mark as attending is shown on your card temporarily and disappears automatically one hour after the event ends. A new event suggestion is seen by the Spark administrator, who can approve, correct or reject it; we tell you about the decision with a notification. An approved event is added to the public catalog without the author's details. You can withdraw a suggestion while it is waiting for a decision.

Location

Location is used to show an approximate distance and to match profiles near you. You can deny access in your system settings, but some discovery features may work less well. We do not store your precise location in your public profile.

Who we share data with

We use providers that process data on our behalf: Google Firebase (hosting, database, sign-in, notifications, analytics, diagnostics), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Apple and Google (sign-in, payments, stores) and Google Mobile Ads (ads). Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area on the basis of standard contractual clauses or other mechanisms provided for by the GDPR. We do not sell personal data and we do not share the content of private messages.

Other users see your public profile: first name, age, photos, bio, interests, goals, city and approximate distance, and — if you choose so — your LGBT+ tag and the events you are attending. Social media links are visible only after a match.

How long we keep data

We keep account data for as long as the account exists. Event attendance is removed one hour after the event ends. After you delete your account we delete the data described in the section below.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, in Settings. After you confirm, we delete your sign-in account, profile, photos, likes, matches, messages and settings, including the LGBT+ tag. A minimal technical record of the request, reports of violations, block identifiers and billing records may be kept only for as long as needed to prevent abuse, meet legal obligations and handle purchases.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent (for example for the LGBT+ tag or for ads) at any time — this does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. You can change your ad consent settings in the app, in the privacy section. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl). Depending on where you live, local law may give you additional privacy rights; write to us and we will handle your request.

Contact

Send questions and requests to access, correct or delete your data to sparkapp@gmail.com.
