Spark Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Spark is meant to be a safe place for adults, built on consent. By using the app you agree to follow the rules below.

Respect and consent

Treat others with respect. Do not harass, threaten or pressure anyone into contact, and do not send unwanted sexual content.

Authenticity

Do not impersonate other people. Use your own photos and true information in your profile.

Prohibited content

Illegal, violent or hateful content, spam, scams, attempts to obtain other people's data by deception and any content that sexualizes minors are prohibited.

Reporting and blocking

You can report or block any profile. We review reports within 24 hours. Reports can lead to account restrictions, removal of content or a permanent ban.

Contact

For safety matters, write to sparkapp@gmail.com.
